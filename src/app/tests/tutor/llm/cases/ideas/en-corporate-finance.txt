Title: Corporate Finance
Discipline: Finance
Level: Master 1
Overview: The course covers how firms value investment projects, choose how to finance them and manage risk.
Topics:
- Investment decisions (Net present value, Internal rate of return)
- The cost of capital (CAPM, WACC)
- Capital structure
- Risk management with derivatives
Learning objectives:
- Value an investment project with discounted cash flows.
- Estimate a firm's weighted average cost of capital.
- Explain how leverage affects a firm's value and risk.
Course outline:
- Week 1 – Time value of money
- Week 2 – Project valuation
- Week 3 – Risk and return
- Week 4 – Cost of capital
- Week 5 – Debt and equity
- Week 6 – Hedging with futures and options
