- "summary": a short overview of the course (2 or 3 sentences): what it covers and what students learn in it, written only from the document's content, adding nothing. null if the document does not allow it.
- "topics": the main topics of the course, identified from its content, outline, chapters or sessions, even when they are not presented as a list; in the course's order. For each: "title" (the document's own heading, as written; only when it is very long, a short version in its own words), "subtopics" (only the few main subtopics the document places under this topic, a few words each, not every point of the outline; otherwise []) and "source" (the heading or line of the document the topic comes from, copied word for word). When the document numbers its themes, chapters, parts or modules (for example "Theme 3: …", "Chapter 2 – …"), each numbered item is one topic, in order, with its heading as "title" and its line as "source": do not group them under broader categories mentioned elsewhere in the text, and do not replace them with such categories. Never two topics with the same title. Only topics present in the document: add nothing. No exams, readings, assignments or review sessions.
